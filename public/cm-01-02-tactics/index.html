CM 01/02 tactics: the classic formations, and why they worked

CM 01/02 is remembered for tactics that broke the match engine. Here is what the classic ones did, and how the same ideas play out in CM Reborn.

The famous CM 01/02 exploits

  • The DMC. In the original, a defensive midfielder covered far more of the pitch than any other role, so one or two DMCs made almost any team solid.
  • 4-2-4 with forward arrows. Four forwards, with the wide midfielders pushed on with arrows, overloaded the defence and scored freely.
  • Through balls and pace. Fast strikers against a high line were almost unstoppable, especially with "run with ball" and "through balls" on.
  • Offside trap and hard tackling. A popular combination that the AI rarely punished.

How tactics work in CM Reborn

CM Reborn has no special rules for any formation. The pitch is split into zones, and the shape of your formation decides where you have more players than the opponent. Four strikers leave the midfield empty, so the other team wins more duels and more of the ball. A player out of position gets worse. The result is that classic ideas still work in the right situation, but nothing wins every match.

  • Overloads matter: attack where the opponent is weak (the assistant shows you which side).
  • Instructions have a price: pressing tires players, a high line invites through balls, and hard tackling brings cards.
  • Player instructions: playmaker, target man, run with ball, long shots, crossing – per player, like in CM 01/02.
  • Test it yourself: the built-in tactics benchmark plays many matches with your tactic and shows the points per game.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best formation in CM 01/02?

In the original, narrow formations with one or two defensive midfielders and the 4-2-4 with arrows were the strongest because of how the match engine worked. In CM Reborn no formation is overpowered – the best one depends on your players and the opponent.

Does the DMC exploit work in CM Reborn?

No. Every role covers its own zones, so a DMC protects the defence but does not cover the whole pitch.
