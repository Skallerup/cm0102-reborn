Play CM 01/02 on Mac, iPad, Chromebook and Android

The original Championship Manager 01/02 is a Windows game. On a Mac you need Wine, a wrapper app or a virtual machine, and on an iPad or a Chromebook it is close to impossible. CM Reborn runs in any modern browser instead.

How to play

  1. Open cm0102reborn.com in Safari, Chrome, Firefox or Edge.
  2. Choose New game, pick a database (the original 2001/02 or 2026/27) and the classic CM 01/02 look.
  3. Optional: add it to your home screen or dock ("Install app") – it then opens like a normal app and works offline.

Works on

  • Mac (Intel and Apple Silicon)
  • iPad and iPhone
  • Chromebook
  • Android phones and tablets
  • Windows and Linux

Your save is stored in the browser on that device. With a free account you can also keep saves in the cloud and continue on another device.

Frequently asked questions

Can I play CM 01/02 on a Mac?

The original needs Windows (or Wine/a virtual machine). CM Reborn, a free CM 01/02 remake, runs directly in Safari or Chrome on any Mac.

Can I play CM 01/02 on iPad?

Yes – open cm0102reborn.com in Safari and add it to the home screen. It works offline too.
