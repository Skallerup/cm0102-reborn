Play CM 01/02 online – free, in your browser

CM Reborn is a free fan remake of Championship Manager 01/02 that runs straight in your browser. Pick the original 2001/02 database or the 2026/27 update, choose the classic CM 01/02 look or a modern one, and you are managing your club in seconds – no installer, no emulator, no patch files.

What you get

  • Two databases: the original CM 01/02 database (patch 3.9.68, season 2001/02, 34,976 players in 161 leagues) and the community 2026/27 update (22,673 players in 38 leagues).
  • The classic CM 01/02 screens: the blue menus, the yellow-on-blue match commentary, Match Overview, the squad screen with selection buttons, Staff Search, Find – and stadium backgrounds on matchdays.
  • A match engine calibrated against the original: we played a full season of the original CM 01/02 and matched goals per game (2.87), home wins, cards, corners and shots on target.
  • Real squad numbers from the database, wonderkids with random potential, transfers, loans, press conferences, board confidence and the sack.
  • Online multiplayer with friends, a fantasy draft league and global rankings.
  • Saves in your browser (and optional cloud saves) – close the tab and continue later. It installs as an app and works offline.
  • Eight languages: English, Dansk, Español, Français, Deutsch, Italiano, Português and Türkçe.

Do I need the original game?

No. CM Reborn is built from scratch and runs on its own. If you still play the original CM 01/02 (it has been free since 2008), you will feel at home: the screens, the database and the way the match plays out are modelled on it.

How is it different from the original?

It runs anywhere (Mac, Windows, Linux, Chromebook, iPad, Android, iPhone), it has an optional modern look, a 2026/27 database, multiplayer and a match engine without the famous exploits – but the classic look and the 2001/02 database are there for the nostalgia.
