Frequently asked questions

Who is the best wonderkid in CM 01/02?

By fixed potential in the 3.9.68 database: Michael Owen (Liverpool, PA 195), Javier Saviola (F.C. Barcelona, PA 195), Tomás Rosicky (BV Borussia Dortmund, PA 190). The cult heroes like Cherno Samba and Ricardo Quaresma have random potential and can become even better.

Why is Cherno Samba so good in CM 01/02?

In the original 3.9.68 database Cherno Samba (Millwall, born 1985) has a random potential code. The game rolls his potential at the start of each save, and it is often very high – which made him a legend.
