CM Reborn – frequently asked questions

CM Reborn is an independent, free fan remake of Championship Manager 01/02 that runs in the browser.

Frequently asked questions

What is CM Reborn?

A free football manager in the browser, built from scratch and inspired by Championship Manager 01/02, with the original 2001/02 database, a 2026/27 database, the classic CM look and a modern look.

Does it cost anything?

No. It is free, with no ads or in-game purchases.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in your browser, and you can install it as an app on phone and desktop. It works offline too.

Where is my game saved?

Automatically in your browser on your device. You can export and import saves, and with a free account keep them in the cloud.

Are the players real?

Yes: 34,960 players in the original CM 01/02 database (2001/02) and 22,673 in the 2026/27 database.

Can I play with friends?

Yes. Create an online game, share the code and run your own clubs in the same world. There is also a fantasy draft league.

Is this the original Championship Manager?

No. It is an independent fan project, not affiliated with Sports Interactive, SEGA or Eidos.

Which languages are supported?

English, Danish, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese and Turkish.
